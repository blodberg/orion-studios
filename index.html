Our apps

TripCache

Every trip has a budget, and every budget has a story. TripCache keeps travel expenses effortless to capture and beautiful to read, offline, on-device, in any currency.

Capture in seconds. Settle up in one tap.

Log an expense in two taps at the gate, on the train, or mid-tapas, with categories, receipts, and split costs included. Spend in yen, kroner, and pesos on the same trip; TripCache converts everything back to your home currency automatically.

When the trip ends, one tap turns it into a clean, shareable report for reimbursements, tax season, or the group chat. Your spending lives on your device and your iCloud, never on our servers. It works fully offline and syncs when you land.

The studio

Small studio. High standards.

Orion Studios is an independent shop that builds a few apps and builds them properly. Sailors once crossed oceans by this constellation; we hold our software to the same idea: something you can rely on, wherever you are.

01

Native or nothing

Every app is written in Swift and SwiftUI, tuned for the platform it lives on. No wrappers, no lowest common denominator. Just iOS apps that feel like iOS.

02

Sweat the last 10%

Haptics, animation curves, empty states, the weight of a tap target. The parts most teams cut are the parts people feel every day.

03

Privacy as a feature

We build products, not audiences. Your data stays yours: on your device, in your iCloud, out of our hands. That's a promise across every app we ship.
